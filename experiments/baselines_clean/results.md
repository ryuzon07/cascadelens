# Clean-data baselines

Generated by `cascadelens experiments baselines-clean`. Scores are percentages with 95% group-bootstrap intervals, averaged per case over each method's seeds.

Supervised and unsupervised methods are separate tables; comparing across them is only descriptive (PRD 7.1). LOFO and LOSO are reported per fold and not pooled (PRD 9.3). The random floor is the expected Top-1 of picking uniformly among a case's services.

Under LOSO the frequency prior has no seen service, so it ties all candidates and its measured Top-1 only reflects the tie-break by service name. Read the random floor instead (PRD 6.4, rule 5). RE1 has no topology, so `rule_upstream` here is a plain max-anomaly ranking.
