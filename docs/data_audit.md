## How fields are derived

- **Labels:** the `<service>_<fault>` directory name, split on the last underscore.
- **run_id:** `<suite>/<service>_<fault>`, shared by all repetitions of that fault.
- **Window:** first and last timestamp of the recording, computed from the `time` column before `inject_time.txt` is read. No direct dependence on `inject_time`.

## Concerns

- **Window tied to inject_time in RE1-OB:** in 124 of 125 cases the injection falls within 2% of 50.0% of the recording, so the window bounds reveal inject_time even though they are computed without it. The window-leak probe (PRD 6.5) must cover this.
- **Window length tracks fault type in RE1-OB** (median seconds: cpu=4200, delay=720, disk=720, loss=720, mem=4200). Recording length alone separates fault types, which is a leak for any model that sees window bounds, and for LOFO. Crop all cases to a common length.
- **Window tied to inject_time in RE1-SS:** in 123 of 125 cases the injection falls within 2% of 50.0% of the recording, so the window bounds reveal inject_time even though they are computed without it. The window-leak probe (PRD 6.5) must cover this.
- Window length varies within RE1-SS: 3 distinct lengths from 606 to 720 s, not by fault type.
- **Window tied to inject_time in RE1-TT:** in 124 of 125 cases the injection falls within 2% of 50.0% of the recording, so the window bounds reveal inject_time even though they are computed without it. The window-leak probe (PRD 6.5) must cover this.
- **Window length tracks fault type in RE1-TT** (median seconds: cpu=960, delay=960, disk=720, loss=960, mem=960). Recording length alone separates fault types, which is a leak for any model that sees window bounds, and for LOFO. Crop all cases to a common length.
- 7 case(s) needed a raw-file repair (see the warnings table): untimed tail rows or a second table on another clock were dropped.
- 67 case(s) hold series with no values on non-label services. They are kept as missing telemetry; per-metric availability will differ across cases, and a model could read that as a collection artifact (mask-feature probe).
- Raw files the loader never reads: simple_data.csv (250).
- Runs hold up to 5 repetitions, which are near-duplicate candidates. Split by run_id, never by case.
- 1 case(s) miss over 10% of their samples.
- No topology for any case. Graph features and P6-P8 need an inferred or hand-built call graph, which would be a separate decision.
